# Release 4.0.0

Date: 2026-11-19. One newsroom for several stations.

Built from the client's requirements: stations that coordinate through chat groups, no way to weigh workloads before asking for more staff, unclear output per station, and the need to see who works on what and export each person's record.

## What is new

- **Stations** (`src/shared/stations.ts`). Nothing is fixed to three stations: an administrator adds and removes them in الإعدادات ← «المحطات وبطاقات الإنتاج», may mark one as the main station (the rest are branches) and decides which stations work together. Each user belongs to one station (`User.stationId`), set in the user form; a user without a station is central management.
- **Production cards** (`src/shared/productionCards.ts`, collection `productionCards`). One card travels with an item between stations: what is needed, who owns each step, delivery and air times, the script, links, and an append-only trail. Finishing a step hands the item to the next person with a note of what is complete and what is missing; a blocked step needs a reason and alerts the decision owner.
- **«مهامي»** on the cards screen and the dashboard: my open steps and steps addressed to my station that I can pick up.
- **«متابعة اليوم»** for the chief editor: what airs today, what is ready, late or blocked, why, and who decides.
- **«الأعباء والأداء»** (`src/shared/workload.ts`): open load per station and person in weighted points (step type weight × complexity), compared per person between stations; output per period with a shared item counted once and each station credited for its part; each employee's record with print/PDF and CSV.
- **Shift handover brief** (`src/shared/handover.ts`), written by the platform from the cards: stuck or late, due next, in progress, finished, colleagues' notes. Copy as text or print.
- **Brief drafts**: the assistant (`BRIEF` mode of `/api/v1/ai/copilot`, when `GEMINI_API_KEY` is set) drafts a short on-air brief from the card and lists what is missing to verify. Nothing is saved until a person saves it; approval stays with the responsible editor.

## Rules enforced on the server

- New permissions: `cards.view`, `cards.work`, `cards.manage`, `workload.view`, `workload.view_all`. Existing databases get them once for the system roles that need them (`NEW_PERMISSION_GRANTS`); custom roles are edited in «المستخدمون والصلاحيات».
- Only `cards.manage` creates, edits, closes, cancels or deletes cards and approves scripts. An assignee changes the status, note, blocked reason and time of their own step only; anyone with `cards.work` may pick up an unassigned step addressed to their station (or to nobody) and add notes.
- A supervisor assigns people inside their station's scope: own station, the main station and linked stations. The main station and users without a station assign anywhere.
- Names, stations, start and completion times, the author of every note and the script approval are stamped by the server (`src/server/cards.ts`). Earlier trail entries cannot be rewritten or removed by anyone. A script approval does not survive a change to the text by a non-supervisor.
- `stationId` is a privileged user field: colleagues cannot move themselves between stations, and an unknown station is rejected.
- Editorial tasks get a server `completedAt`, so they count in output per period.

## How work is measured

- Card step: weight of its type (1-10, editable) × complexity (1 simple, 2 medium, 3 complex).
- Outside cards: editorial task 2 points, department request 2 (urgent 4), news story written 2. A story produced through a card is counted by the card only.
- Station level: open points per person compared with the average of the staffed stations: above 1.5× is «حمل مرتفع», below 0.5× «طاقة متاحة».

## Boundaries

- Workload and output are computed in the browser from synced data. News older than `NEWS_ACTIVE_DAYS` (90 by default) is no longer synced, so periods longer than that undercount news written outside cards. Cards, tasks and requests are complete.
- Tasks finished before this release have no `completedAt` and are not counted in period output.
- Station scope limits what supervisors see in «الأعباء والأداء» and whom they may assign. Cards themselves are readable by everyone with `cards.view`: it is one shared newsroom.
- Interview transcription is not part of this release: it needs an audio pipeline and a transcription provider chosen by the client.
- The brief assistant was checked for permissions and its "not configured" answer; it was not exercised against the live Gemini service in this environment (no key).
